About your teacher

Fifteen years of teaching the Quran with patience and care

Assalamu alaikum. I am Qari M. Asif, and for 15 years I have been teaching the Quran to students of all ages. Today I teach students in Australia, the USA, the UK, Pakistan and other countries, all through live online classes.

Every class is one-to-one on Google Meet, so each student gets my full attention. We move at a pace that suits the student, from the first letters of Noorani Qaida to fluent reading with Tajweed and Hifz.

I have a special place in my heart for children. Lessons are kept simple, patient and enjoyable, so young students look forward to their class. Adults are always welcome too, even if you are starting from the very beginning.
